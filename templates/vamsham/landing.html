FAQ

Questions families ask

Who can see our family details?

Only members your committee has approved. Every family has its own private space — other families on Vamsham can’t see yours.

How does a member join?

Open the family app, choose “I’m new — join my family”, enter a phone number and the SMS code. The committee approves the request, and the member is in.

Is face sign-in safe?

Yes. It is optional, needs the person’s agreement, and stores only a face code — not the photo. Anyone can remove their face data from Settings.

Can elders without a smartphone be in the tree?

Of course. The committee adds everyone — with or without a phone — and those who passed away are kept in the tree with love.

How do we start Vamsham for our family?

Get in touch with the Vamsham team. We create your family’s space and committee sign-in, then you add branches and members from the dashboard.
